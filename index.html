Japanese investors' foreign bond buying — by investor type

Residents' monthly net purchase of foreign long-term debt securities, by investor type — the mirror image of the JSDA JGB-by-player data: same players, but buying abroad instead of at home. Positive = net buying of foreign bonds (yen outflow); negative = net selling / repatriation. Source: MoF International Transactions in Securities (対外証券投資 中長期債), monthly; USD converted at each month's USDJPY.

Trust a/c (pension) is the big structural outbound bid (GPIF & pension money); banks (banking a/c) swing with the yen-hedging / repatriation cycle; life insurers' foreign-bond appetite reads against their JGB super-long demand on the JSDA tab.
