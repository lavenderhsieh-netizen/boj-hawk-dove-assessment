### New "BOJ Balance Sheet" tab

Positioned in the nav right after "Intervention" (doc's own framing: MOF issuance → BOJ purchases/runoff → private-sector absorption — Balance Sheet sits with the policy/operations tabs, not buried at the end).

**`fetch_boj_balance_sheet.py`** (mirrors `fetch_mof_flows.py`'s house style: dict-of-series output, idempotent overwrite, `meta` block with source/as-of/generated-at) pulls real official data from three sources, no scraping-fragile HTML parsing required for the core series:

- **DB=BS01** "Bank of Japan Accounts" via the **BOJ Time-Series Data Search API** (`https://www.stat-search.boj.or.jp/api/v1/getDataCode`, no API key required, launched Feb 2026 — discovered via the unofficial `bojapi`/`boj-api-client` R/Python packages' docs, not obvious from the BOJ website itself). Clean monthly end-of-month series back to 1998/04, sliced to 2012-01 onward per the doc's ask (175 months as of this write). Fields: total assets, JGS holdings + share of assets, corporate bonds, ETFs, J-REITs, loans, FX assets (asset side); banknotes, current-account deposits, government deposits, repo liabilities, other deposits (liability side).
- **DB=FM05** "Ordinary Government Securities" + "Treasury Discount Bills" Amount Outstanding (same API) — denominator for "BOJ share of total JGB outstanding" (~40-43% latest, lags BS01 by ~1 month).
- **DB=MD06** "Sources of Changes in Current Account Balances and Market Operations" — `MASDM58` (Outright Purchases of JGBs) and `MASDM5A@` (Outright Purchases of T-Bills), monthly since 1960/1999. This is the doc's Section C "JGB purchase operations" ask — a genuine official monthly gross-purchases series (not broken out by maturity bucket; that detail only exists in BOJ's daily operation-result HTML pages at `boj.or.jp/en/statistics/boj/fm/ope/`, which are not a clean scrapable time series and were not built this session).
- **Cabinet Office ESRI** Quarterly Estimates of GDP CSV (nominal, original series) — denominator for total-assets/GDP. Scraped via the EN release-index page (`esri.cao.go.jp/en/sna/sokuhou/sokuhou_top.html`) to find the current release folder, then that release's own menu page to find its actual CSV link — do NOT try to construct the CSV URL from the folder name directly, the file-code suffix doesn't map predictably (e.g. folder `qe261_2` → file `gaku-mg2612.csv`, not `gaku-mg261_2.csv`). Unit is ¥10bn (十億円), divide by 1000 for ¥tn.

Writes `boj_balance_sheet.json`. Derived metrics computed in the fetch script itself (same convention as `fetch_natural_rate.py`, not client-side JS): MoM change in JGB holdings, 12m change, 3-month annualised balance-sheet contraction rate `((assets_t/assets_t-3)^4 - 1)*100`, total assets/GDP (quarterly GDP annualised ×4), BOJ/total-JGB-outstanding share. GDP and FM05 series both lag BS01 by 1-3 months — the `latest` block falls back to the most recent non-null value for those two ratios (with its own `_asof` field) rather than showing null.

Tab content: "why this matters" framing card (doc's own line, verbatim), 6-KPI row, then charts — total BOJ assets since 2012 (line), JGB holdings since 2012 (line), monthly change in JGB holdings (bar), current-account/reserve balances (line), monthly gross JGB purchase operations (stacked bar, JGB+T-Bill), asset composition for the latest month (donut + table), 12-month change in JGB holdings (bar), BOJ share of total JGB outstanding (line), total assets/GDP (line), 3-month annualised contraction rate (bar) — plus a sourcing/caveat footer with links and the exact cross-check note below.

Keyword search "ソブリン" under category BP01 surfaces the exact series codes: `BP01'BPPI6D1N{XX}` = "対外証券投資 ソブリン債/合計/{country}/ネット" (Total sovereign bonds, country, Net) for XX ∈ {US,CA,AU,DE,FR,IT,NL,GB,DK,CH,HK,SE} plus `AOT` (その他/Others) — exactly matches this project's existing 12-country + others structure, no remapping needed.
